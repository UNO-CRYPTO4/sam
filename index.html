01 — First, I’m sorry.

First, I’m sorry. ❤️

I’m sorry for my part in everything that has happened lately— for the moments I got upset, for the things I said or how I handled certain things, and for the ways I may have hurt you.

I don’t want to hide behind the fact that I was hurt too. I know I have my own part to take responsibility for, and I’m genuinely sorry.

“

If we both get easily upset, is this good for our relationship?

”

I’ve been thinking about the question you asked me. And honestly, I think it’s a question we need to take seriously.

02 — What we have

Not because I think what we have is bad. In fact, I think the opposite.

I think we’ve become so important to each other that sometimes our emotions get louder than they should, and little things begin to carry the weight of much bigger things.

So maybe we need to go back to the drawing board.

04 — The original drawing

The foundation.

But to go back to the original drawing.

The one where two people met because they wanted to study, help each other, grow academically, and become better.

The friendship that came out of that was a beautiful addition— not something either of us had to force.

I still value that friendship. I value you.

Where it started

Two people trying to become better.

That part of our story still matters to me.

05 — Remembering what matters

I don’t want us to get so caught up in expectations, misunderstandings, emotions and repeated little fights that we lose sight of the very thing that made this friendship worth having in the first place.

I don’t want to invalidate what you feel, and I don’t want to invalidate what I feel either.

I just think we need to learn how to carry those feelings without allowing them to dictate everything between us.

Maybe the reset is simply this:

Acknowledge. Forgive. Learn. Adjust. Start again.

07 — Putting it down

I’m putting down my side of the fight.

I don’t want one difficult season to rewrite the entire story of our friendship.

So, for whatever it is worth, I’m putting down my side of the fight.

✕ No more keeping receipts.
✕ No more trying to prove who was more hurt.
✕ No more turning every disagreement into a referendum on whether we are okay.

One difficult season doesn't have to become the whole story.

08 — So yes, Sampson.

Let’s go back to the drawing board.

I don’t expect us to magically never upset each other again. We’re human.

But I do think we can become better at handling each other.

And maybe when we’re both calm, we can figure out together what this friendship needs from both of us going forward.

Because I’d rather fix how we’re doing this than lose something genuinely good because we didn’t know when to pause and reset.
